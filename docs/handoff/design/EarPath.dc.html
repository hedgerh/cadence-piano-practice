Ear training

The app plays, you name it, then you play it back. Five stages, each building on the last — from two notes to a whole key.

How a level works
1Ten questions. Replay the sound as often as you like — no penalty.
2Name it. Wrong answers show you the right one on the keys.
3Play it back on your keyboard to lock it in (+5 XP each).
48 of 10 passes the level. Misses come back in tomorrow's review.
