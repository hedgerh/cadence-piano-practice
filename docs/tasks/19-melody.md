# 19 — Melody tracks: Arpeggios & chord tones, Melody echo

**Status:** done on `main` (2026-10-17). E2E and screenshots are pending the owner's go-ahead (they open app windows).

These are roadmap phases 3–4 and the top two "Next up" items on the track backlog. They put chord tones under the hand and train playing back what you hear: the two habits improvising rests on.

## Inputs

- `docs/roadmap.md` phases 3–4.
- HANDOFF §4 (Match union: `sequence`) and §5 (sequences note by note, a wrong note resets silently).
- engine.md §2.2 (phrases, seeded).

## Deliverables

- **Engine**:
  - `sequence.shape`: the whole run in any octave, same shape.
  - `Level.noun` and the step nouns `arpeggio` and `phrase`, threaded through `SessionDto.stepNoun`.
  - Practice steps play their `play` prompt by themselves (once per visit; "Play it again") or their `hint` demo on request ("Hear it"), via `useStepSound`.
  - Chips on sequence steps that have `parts`.
  - The three-octave keyboard for `shape` levels.
- **Content** (a new "Melody" group, 12 major keys each):
  - **Arpeggios & chord tones**, with a lesson. Steps: I, IV, V and vi up to the octave and back; then the roots, 3rds and 5ths of I–IV–V–I; then the 3rds of I–V–vi–IV.
  - **Melody echo**, with a lesson. 8 phrases per key in 4 tiers, generated by `echoPhrase`: 3 notes stepwise → 4 notes → 5 with 3rds → 5 with one leap.
  - Validator: shapes are at least 2 notes and stay in C3..B5; no repeated phrase within a level; chip parts per note of a sequence; nouns only on sequence levels.
- **Copy**:
  - Plan: "C major · arpeggios and chord tones" and "C major · 8 phrases to echo".
  - Complete screen: "Every arpeggio and chord tone, in your hands." and "All eight phrases, played back by ear."
  - Practice: captions per kind of step, and "All eight by ear. Nice."

## Acceptance

- `pnpm check` green. The content snapshot and validator cover all 24 new levels.
- An arpeggio played an octave higher passes, and a note in the wrong octave restarts it.
- Chord-tone chips light one per note.
- An echo prompt plays by itself once per visit, and a demo only on request.

## Out of scope

- Minor keys.
- Left-hand chords under arpeggios.
- Grading rhythm.
- A "target tone" match (any chord tone at each change). That belongs with Jam.

- None from the design files: these screens reuse the Practice layout.
